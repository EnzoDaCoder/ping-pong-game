Player 1 breaks. Aim with the mouse, hold click, pull backward, and release.
Mouse / Touch = Aim • Hold + Pull Back = Power • Release = Shoot
Side Spin = Left / Right English • Draw = Backspin • Follow = Topspin
Rules: The table starts open. After the break, the first legally pocketed solid or stripe assigns the groups. Hit your own group first. After contact, a ball must reach a rail or be pocketed. Fouls give the other player ball-in-hand. Once all seven of your balls are gone, call a pocket and legally sink the 8-ball to win. Sinking the 8-ball early, on a foul, or in the wrong called pocket loses the rack.
